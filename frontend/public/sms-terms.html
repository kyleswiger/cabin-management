SMS Terms & Privacy

Text message reminders for members of this private property-management site.

What this service is

This is a private, invite-only site used by one family to coordinate a shared property: booking visits, tracking household supplies, and keeping up with maintenance and yardwork. Accounts are created by an administrator. There is no public signup, and the site sells nothing.

What messages we send

Operational reminders only, tied to your own use of the site: an upcoming reservation, supplies that are low or out before a visit, and yardwork that is due. We never send marketing, promotional, or third-party messages.

How you opt in

Signing in and going to your profile page, entering your own mobile number, and checking the consent box — which is unchecked by default. No message is ever sent to a number that has not been through that step. We record the date and time you consented. Changing your phone number clears your consent, and you must opt in again for the new number.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with your reservations and the state of the property, up to about one message per day. Message and data rates may apply — your mobile carrier, not this site, may charge you for receiving text messages.

HELP and STOP

Reply HELP to any message for help. Reply STOP to unsubscribe; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages. You can also turn texts off at any time by unchecking the consent box on your profile page, which stops them immediately.

Privacy

Your phone number is used for one purpose: sending you the reminders described above. We do not sell, rent, or share phone numbers with anyone, and we do not share them with third parties for marketing. Numbers are stored with the rest of your profile and are visible only to you and the site's administrators. Removing your number from your profile removes it from the site.

Contact

Reply HELP to any message, or contact the family administrator who invited you to the site.
